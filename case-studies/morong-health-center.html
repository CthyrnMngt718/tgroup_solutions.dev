Morong Health Center Management System

A healthcare management system focused on organizing patient-related information, appointments, inventory, monitoring, reporting, and administrative workflows.

HealthcareWeb-BasedPHPMySQLBootstrapJavaScript

Problem / Operational Need

Health-center operations involve multiple types of information and recurring administrative tasks. The system is designed to bring patient-related records, appointments, inventory, monitoring, and reporting into a more structured digital workflow.

Solution Direction

A centralized web-based management system that organizes key operational modules and provides staff with a clearer place to manage day-to-day information.

System Scope

  • Patient-related record organization
  • Appointment scheduling workflow
  • Inventory management
  • Monitoring and reporting
  • Administrative data access

Technical Focus

The project demonstrates database-driven web development using PHP and MySQL, with responsive interfaces built with Bootstrap and JavaScript-enhanced interactions.
