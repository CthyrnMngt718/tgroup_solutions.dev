Small Business Management System

A management-system concept for products, transactions, customer records, monitoring, and everyday operational activities.

BusinessManagement ConceptWeb-BasedDatabase

Problem / Operational Need

Small businesses often track products, sales, customer information, and daily operations across separate tools or manual records, making monitoring and reporting harder.

Solution Direction

A centralized management-system concept that brings product, transaction, customer, and operational information into one structured workflow.

System Scope

  • Product and inventory management
  • Transaction monitoring
  • Customer record organization
  • Operational dashboards
  • Reporting-oriented data views

Technical Focus

The concept focuses on relational database design, CRUD workflows, role-oriented dashboards, operational reporting, and a practical web-based interface.
